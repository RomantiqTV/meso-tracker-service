Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what Meso Tracker (“we”, “us”), operated by Developer Griff, collects when you use the Meso Tracker desktop app and the mesotracker.pro website, why, and what your choices are. Short version: the app reads your game screen on your own PC and keeps what it reads there, unless you choose to switch on the optional “Help improve Meso Tracker” setting (section 2). We only receive the small amount of information needed to run your subscription and licence.

1. What the app does on your PC

  • Meso Tracker reads the pixels of your MapleStory Classic window (screen capture, text recognition and template matching) to work out EXP, mesos, items picked up and supplies used. It never reads the game’s memory or files, never touches the game’s network traffic, and never sends keyboard or mouse input to the game.
  • Screenshots, the numbers it reads, your session totals and your settings stay on your computer, in the app’s own folder under your user profile. They are not uploaded to us, with one optional exception you control: see “Help improve Meso Tracker” below.
  • If you turn on the optional recording features for troubleshooting, the recordings are also saved only on your PC. They are off by default.

2. What we collect

Account and billing

  • Your email address, which you give us at checkout.
  • A Stripe customer ID, your subscription status (for example active or cancelled) and the date your paid period ends.
  • Your card or payment details are handled entirely by Stripe. They never reach our servers and we cannot see them.

Free trial

  • If you start the free trial we store your email address, a normalised form of it (so the same mailbox cannot start a second trial), the IP address you signed up from, and the start and end dates of your trial. No card or payment details are involved.
  • We also remember the hashed device ID of any PC that has run a trial, so a second trial is not started on the same PC. This is used only to limit repeat trials.

Licence and device

  • Your licence key is shown to you once, by email. We store only a keyed one-way hash of it, plus its last four characters so we can help you in support.
  • A hashed device ID. The app derives it from your Windows machine identifier and system drive serial number and sends only the hash, so we cannot recover the originals. It ties your licence to one device at a time.
  • An activation log: when the app activates, re-checks or deactivates, we record the event type, the hashed device ID, your IP address, the app version and the time. We use it to prevent key sharing and to diagnose problems.

Help improve Meso Tracker (optional, off by default)

When the app cannot read a line of the pickup feed (an item name or quantity), it can send us a small picture of just that one line so we can train the readers on more players’ games. The app asks you once, with “No” as the default, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings > Account. If you do not say Yes, nothing is sent.

  • What is sent: a picture a few rows tall of the pickup text, the app version, your game window size, and a short note on why the read failed (including the text the app managed to read from that line, if any).
  • What is never sent: screenshots, chat, your character name, the map, your email address or your licence key. We store the sender only as a keyed one-way hash of your licence, so a picture cannot be traced back to your account without our secret key, and we do not do that.
  • Your control: switch it off at any time and nothing more is sent. “Delete my contributed data” in Settings > Account deletes everything your licence contributed from our server. Pictures are otherwise kept for as long as we are using them to improve the readers.

Update checks

Each time the app starts it asks our server which version is newest. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the server.

Website

  • mesotracker.pro does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and its pages load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
  • Checkout and the billing portal are hosted by Stripe, under Stripe’s own privacy policy.
  • Our server software and hosting provider keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, time and requested address) for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the app: confirm your subscription is active, bind your licence to one device and deliver updates.
  • To take payment, send your receipt, licence key and service emails, and handle refunds and support.
  • To protect the service against key sharing, fraud and abuse (including repeated free trials), and to keep it secure.
  • If you opt in: to train and test the app’s item and number readers. Contributed pictures are used for nothing else and are not shared or sold.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information, we do not show advertising, and we do not build profiles of you. We only email you about your purchase, your licence, security and important changes to the service (for example a required update).

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Stripe – payments, subscriptions and the billing portal.
  • Resend – sending transactional email (receipt, licence key, notices).
  • Render – hosting the licence server and this website.
  • Neon – hosting the database (licence records and, only if you opt in, the pickup-line pictures you contribute).
  • GitHub – hosting the installer download; GitHub sees the IP address of the downloader.
  • Microsoft – if you install Meso Tracker from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles that download and updates under its own privacy statement.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and the security of the service.

5. Where data is stored

Our providers may store and process data outside your country, including in the United States. We rely on these providers’ security and data-protection commitments for those transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account, subscription and licence records while your account is active and for as long afterwards as we need for billing, tax, accounting, fraud prevention and dispute handling. Activation logs are kept for the same reasons. You can ask us to delete your data (see below); we will do so unless we are required to keep a record.

7. Security

Traffic between the app, the website and our server uses HTTPS. Licence keys are stored only as keyed hashes, and payment events from Stripe are cryptographically verified. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal. Email support@mesotracker.pro from the address on your account and we will respond within a reasonable time (we aim for 30 days). Deleting your account also ends your licence.

If you are in Australia you may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you think we have mishandled your information. If you are in the EU or UK you may contact your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Meso Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. A subscription must be paid for by an adult or with a parent or guardian’s permission.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed; if a change is significant we will tell subscribers by email.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: support@mesotracker.pro.
