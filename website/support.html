Support

Quick answers and how to reach us.

Need a hand? Here are the quick answers, and how to reach us.

Contact

Email support@mesotracker.pro from the address you subscribed with. Please include your app version (shown in the app) and, if something is not tracking correctly, what you were doing and your game window size.

I did not get my licence key

It is emailed right after checkout and usually arrives within a minute or two. Check your spam folder; if it is not there, email us and we will resend it. We only keep a one-way hash of your key, so we cannot read it back to you, but we can issue a new one.

Manage or cancel my subscription

Use Manage billing in the app, or the link in your emails. See Refunds & Cancellation.

Moving to a new PC

A licence works on one device at a time. When you sign in on a new PC, the app offers to deactivate the old one and use the new one.

Installing on Windows

  • Meso Tracker supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 and is best tested at 1366×768, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 game window sizes.
  • The downloaded installer is not signed by a commercial publisher certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • If Windows 11’s Smart App Control blocks it with no “Run anyway” option, install Meso Tracker from the Microsoft Store instead, where one is available, or turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security → App & browser control.
  • Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum on its GitHub release page so you can confirm your download is intact.

The numbers look wrong

  • Make sure the game’s chat/feed panel that shows pickups is visible and not covered by another window.
  • If you changed the game’s resolution or switched between windowed and fullscreen, the app adjusts within a second or two; if it does not, restart it.
  • Update to the latest version, since game updates can change what the app reads.
