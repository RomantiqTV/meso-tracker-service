Kerning Party Quest (KPQ): the level 21-30 technique, precisely

Every stage, King Slime's real numbers, and whether it actually beats grinding.

Kerning Party Quest is the first party quest most MapleStory Classic characters run, and it gets recommended constantly - but most write-ups either skip the mechanics or oversell the EXP. Here's exactly what it requires, what each stage does, and an honest comparison against just grinding a good map in the same level range.

Requirements

  • Level 21 or higher. No job restriction.
  • A party of exactly four. The party leader starts the quest by talking to Lakelis near the sewer entrance in Kerning City.
  • Available on channels 3 through 12. Multiple parties can run it at the same time on those channels.
  • Your party has 30 minutes to clear it once it starts.

The five stages

Each stage is a self-contained puzzle or clear. Get them wrong and you waste time inside your 30-minute window - not fail the run outright, in most cases.

  1. Ligators and Cloto. 22 Ligators drop coupons. Cloto asks each non-leader a question and expects the matching number of coupons back (for example, 10 coupons for a first job advancement question). Since Cloto only questions non-leaders, the leader needs the party to come back through this stage a second time if the leader also needs to answer later - plan for that rather than being surprised by it.
  2. Ropes. Three of your four members hang on three of four ropes, leaving exactly one empty. There are four valid arrangements - if your first guess is wrong, rotate systematically rather than guessing randomly.
  3. Kittens. Three members stand on three of five platforms (ten valid combinations exist). Commonly shared "complete" combination lists are missing at least one valid arrangement, so if you've exhausted a list you found online without success, that's likely why - try arrangements the list doesn't cover.
  4. Barrels. Three members stand on three of six barrels (20 valid combinations). The largest search space of the four puzzle stages - expect this one to take the longest on a blind run.
  5. The clear stage. Six Jr. Neckis, three Curse Eyes, and King Slime. Jr. Neckis have high avoidability, so low-accuracy characters will whiff more than expected here - don't mistake that for bad luck. Each kill type drops a coupon; the leader collects all ten and hands them to Cloto to finish.

King Slime, precisely

King Slime is level 32 with 16,820 HP, worth 2,400 EXP on the kill. He splits into 11 ordinary Slimes on death, so clear those before they bury your party. He's reported to knock back around 1,750 damage, and the community's standard tip is to jump just before his ground-slam animation finishes to avoid it.

What you actually get

  • Stage-completion EXP for each of the five stages, plus 2,400 EXP from King Slime.
  • A chance at Squishy Shoes (level 28) from King Slime's drop table.
  • A Companion's Magic Box for every member who finishes.
  • A one-time follow-up, Proof of Companionship, worth a reported 1,870 EXP, 687 mesos, 1 fame, and a random Intermediate Earring scroll.
  • After the party clears, one member can stay behind in the bonus room (1st Accompaniment) for roughly 20-25 minutes before being sent out - useful if your party splits up afterward.

Does it actually beat grinding? Here's the honest comparison

This is where most guides go quiet. Community EXP/hr modeling puts KPQ's bonus room at roughly 462,858 EXP/hr solo or duo. In the same 21-30 level range, a good uncontested grind map - Ant Tunnel III, for example - is modeled at well over 1,000,000 EXP/hr solo. On raw EXP/hr alone, a good open map beats the KPQ bonus room by a wide margin.

So why run KPQ at all? Three real reasons, none of which are "it's faster":

  • Guaranteed, bursty EXP. 2,400 EXP from one boss kill plus stage completions is a solid lump sum on a fresh level-21 character, before you've found a map that isn't packed with other players.
  • It doesn't compete for a map. If every good 21-25 map is full when you log in, KPQ is EXP you don't have to fight server population for.
  • The gear and the one-time quest. Squishy Shoes and the Proof of Companionship reward only exist here - you can't grind your way to them.

The honest takeaway: treat KPQ as a reliable supplement and a gearing stop, not your main leveling method once you actually have access to an open grind map.
