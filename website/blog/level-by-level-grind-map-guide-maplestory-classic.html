Level 1 through 70, ranked by modeled solo EXP/hr - not vibes.

"Best map" lists are everywhere and most just name a map with no number behind it. Figures are community-sourced estimates, not official Nexon data, and will drift as the population and balance shift - treat the ranking as more reliable than the exact numbers.

Reading this table correctly

Three things matter more than memorizing map names:

  • "Max EXP/hr" assumes you have the map to yourself. On a populated server, the second and third entry in each bracket matter as much as the first - they're your backup when your first choice is camped.
  • A higher-level monster isn't automatically better EXP. Dangerous Valley II at 61-70 is modeled well below Sleepy Dungeon V, a map you can run from level 56 - the monster's level range doesn't guarantee it scales your EXP/hr with it.
  • Party math changes the comparison entirely. A party splits each kill's EXP and adds a bonus on top - but modeling suggests six same-level players need roughly four to five times the kill count of one solo player just to match that solo player's rate. Parties only come out ahead on maps with enough monsters that one player physically couldn't clear them solo.

A table like this tells you where to start. It can't tell you what your character, with your gear and your playstyle, is actually earning on the map you picked tonight - that's a live number, not a community average.
