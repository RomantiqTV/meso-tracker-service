Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 20 November 2026

We want this to be simple and fair. This page is part of our Terms of Use.

Free trial

The 5-day free trial needs no card, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing is charged when it ends. The app simply stops until you choose to subscribe. The 7-day cancellation window below starts from your first payment, not from the trial.

Cancel any time

You can cancel your subscription at any time, without giving a reason. Use the Manage billing option in the app, or the billing link in any of our emails, to open the billing portal and cancel. Once you cancel you will not be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

7-day cancellation

If you are not happy, tell us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund that payment in full, no questions asked. This applies to the first purchase of any plan (monthly, 6-month or yearly).

After the first 7 days

Outside the 7-day window we do not normally give refunds or pro-rata credit for the unused part of a billing period that has already started, because the Service has been available to you for that period. If you cancel, you keep using the Service until that period ends. We will always consider a request if something went wrong (for example you were charged after cancelling, charged twice, or the Service was not usable for a long time), so please contact us.

Your consumer rights

Nothing on this page limits any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer law, including a remedy if the Service has a major failure or is not as described.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@mesotracker.pro from the address on your account and include the date of the charge. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.
